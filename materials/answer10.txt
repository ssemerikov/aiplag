Guidelines for Creating Accessible and Informative Research Network Visualizations

Guidelines for Accessible Visualizations:

    Color Contrast: Ensure sufficient color contrast (minimum ratios of 3:1 or 4.5:1 depending on text size) to accommodate individuals with visual impairments .
    Structural Elements: Use patterns or shapes to differentiate data for colorblind readers, optimizing font styles and sizes for readability .
    Alternative Text: Provide concise, descriptive alternative text for screen readers to improve accessibility for all users .
    User-Centered Design: Apply user-centered design approaches, paying attention to information architecture and selecting the right type of representation and visual encoding .
    Interactive Features: Incorporate interactive elements that allow users to engage with the data dynamically, enhancing their understanding and retention .
