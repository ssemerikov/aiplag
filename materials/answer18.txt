    Traditional vs. AI-Powered Methods:

    Transformer-Based Models:
        Recent advancements include transformer-based models like multi-qa-mpnet-base-dot-v1, roberta-large-openai-detector, and xlm-roberta-large, which excel in semantic, AI-generated, and multilingual plagiarism detection .
        These models achieve up to 95% accuracy in real-time detection, outperforming traditional methods .

    Citation-Based Detection:
        Citation-based plagiarism detection has been shown to be more effective than text-based methods in identifying strong paraphrasing, translation, and idea plagiarism .

    Software Comparisons:

    Advantages:
