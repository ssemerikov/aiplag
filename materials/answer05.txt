AI Plagiarism in Computer Science and Programming Education

    Detection Methods: Text distance algorithms and hierarchical clustering can effectively distinguish between AI-generated and human-written code submissions with over 90% accuracy . Some educators advocate for integrating AI tools into courses to prepare students for future jobs, while others prefer banning them to maintain traditional teaching methods .
    Challenges: AI-generated code presents issues related to code quality, maintainability, and security, along with ethical concerns like ownership and plagiarism . The over-reliance on AI tools can hinder the development of fundamental programming skills .

AI Plagiarism in ESL/EFL Contexts and Language Learning

Key Findings:

    Impact on Learning: ChatGPT enhances learning outcomes in ESL/EFL education by improving writing skills, grammar proficiency, and speaking fluency, but raises concerns about academic dishonesty and dependency on AI tools . AI-driven plagiarism is attributed to students' lack of original ideas, poor learning attitudes, and linguistic competencies .
    Teacher Responses: Teachers advocate for increased regulations, AI-based plagiarism detectors, and education on responsible AI use to address academic dishonesty . Personalized language learning through AI tools like ChatGPT can support critical thinking and genuine learning motivation .
    Student Perceptions: Students worry about hindering their own writing skills and view submitting AI-generated text as academic dishonesty. They believe AI-written works are detectable and support the use of plagiarism detectors .

AI Plagiarism in STEM vs. Humanities Disciplines

Key Findings:

    STEM Education: In programming courses, AI tools like GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT can generate correct solutions and explain code, raising concerns about cheating and the need for new detection methods . Educators are divided on whether to ban or integrate these tools into their teaching .
    Humanities Education: In language learning, AI tools like ChatGPT provide personalized feedback and enhance learning outcomes but also pose risks of academic dishonesty and dependency . Educators emphasize the need for ethical guidelines and structured implementation to mitigate these risks .
    Detection and Adaptation: Both STEM and humanities educators are exploring AI-based plagiarism detectors and adapting their teaching methods to address the challenges posed by AI tools .
